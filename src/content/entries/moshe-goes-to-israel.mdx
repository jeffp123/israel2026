The Peck family trip to Israel started on July 5th with Moshe, who signed up to be part of the NCSY Hatzalah Rescue trip to Israel, with friends from West Hartford.

This would be our third trip to Israel as a family (2nd for the younger children), but the first time that we would go in stages, with Moshe leading the path!

We headed to New Haven in the morning to get the train.

<Photos glob="/src/assets/2026-07-05/to-new-haven/*.jpg" />

Sabba and Savta would meet us on the train. As it turned out, this was one of the busiest days to be taking a train since it was both the day after the Fourth of July and also the day of the World Cup in New Jersey. It was a very cramped train ride with a few different stops to get to Newark airport.

<Photos glob="/src/assets/2026-07-05/to-the-airport/*.jpg" />

We arrived right on time, although some people had gotten there earlier. Once Moshe found his group he was ready to go! He relunctantly accepted some hugs and joined his group.

<Photos glob="/src/assets/2026-07-05/at-the-airport/*.jpg" />

After dropping Moshe off and hanging out at the airport for a little while, we took a different train back (because of the changes due to the World Cup) and ended up in the World Train Center, which none of us have been to since it was rebuilt.

<Figure variant="framed" src={import('/src/assets/2026-07-05/191136.jpg')} alt="World Trade Center" caption="At the World Train Center after dropping off Moshe" />

We headed back home and checked Moshe's flight as he started our Peck Family in Israel 2026 adventures.
