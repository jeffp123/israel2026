My journal is especially sparse today, so I will piece together what I remember from the nine sentences that I wrote and some exchanges of messages with Ben Skoler that day.

## Moshe is Back

Well, the big news of today was that Moshe was back!

On Moshe's Hatzalah trip, he did so much, with almost no down time, not much time for sleep, just constant doing. The trip involved a week or so of training in CPR and other life saving skills and taking tests, and doing drills including a very intense one that simulated a masss casualty event. And then in the evenings, they would go on trips. And they would stay up late and get up early the next day. As Moshe described, they didn't seem to factor in realistic travel time for many of the events, which ultimately cut into sleep or relaxation time. And towards the latter half of the experience they were going on on real patrols and real calls! So, mix in some actual responding to emergency calls, and it was a really intense five (or so) weeks.

I specifically said that Moshe is welcome to sleep as much as he wants, to eat on whatever schedule he likes, and is welcome to just sit and play videogames. Yair helped him get set up with playing Deltarune (I think it was Deltarune...) and I am pretty sure he had some Bamba and just spent the day chilling in the apartment, getting the first real "break" in over five weeks.

## Shopping with Nuriya

After the previous very busy day and Nuriya still seemingly on an American sleep schedule, she woke up late. We took another trip to the Supersol near Prima Kings, stopping at the fountain along the way.

<Photos glob="/src/assets/2026-08-05/143957-fountain-plaza/*.jpg" />

We had fun shopping, this time so much more experienced about every aspect of the process, from which bus to take, to remembering that we need to scan our receipt to exit the store. It felt nice that this activity that felt new just a few days prior now felt familiar.

<Photos glob="/src/assets/2026-08-05/150453-supermarket/*.jpg" />

After we got back, I did some cleanup around the apartment and laundry.

Moshe was set up to sleep on the couch. We talked about moving things around so he could have a section of the living room, but I think he was comfortable on the couch where it was. (I actually don't remember what we ended up doing. We talked about him switching rooms with Yair, but I am pretty sure Moshe was just so tired after the trip that the couch in the main living room space was fine.)

We would be moving out of this apartment in less than a week, for the second half of the trip. And yet, it was all starting to feel so familiar and comfortable here.

## Making My Way to the Kotel

I hung out with everybody that afternoon. I remember telling Yair that he needs to do more planks, after Rifka gave me that message the day before, which ended up taking some time to convey. I don't know what Yair, Nuriya, or Moshe did the rest of that afternoon.

I do remember that I decided to venture to the kotel, but without the help of Google Maps. For some reason I thought that if I stay on the light rail on Jaffa Street it would go straight to the Old City. It turns out that I was wrong and it actually heads in the direction of Ramat Eshkol.

I ended up getting off at a familiar spot for me, in Maalot Dafne, near Ohr Somayach!

I went to Ohr Somayach 20+ years ago, and visitted back in 2013 on Simhat Torah (with Binyomin Adler). I decided to stop in. The security guard asked me lots of questions, since it was Ben Hazman (break in the couple weeks before Elul). Apparently there were only a few people there. I actually had an interesting time at the door because once the security guard buzzed me in, I pulled on the door and it wouldn't open. He said something to me in Hebrew and we tried again a few times. Then, he finally got up and _pushed_ the door open for me. Whoops.

The building was all brand new, totally different than how I remember it years ago. I walked around, peaked in various rooms where a few people were learning Torah over the break.

There was a screen and it said that today was the yahrzeit for Rabbi Berel Wein. We have a book that Nuriya loves called "Buy Green Bananas". I thought it was special that I happened to come into the building where (I am pretty sure) he used to teach and it was his yahrzeit.

## Actually to the Kotel

After that, I realized I'm in the same general area as Reuven K. We texted, but it was not a good time to get together, so I made my way towards the kotel, again trying to navigate with minimally looking at my phone and just getting a sense of where I am going. I got to a bus stop along route 60, but did not feel like waiting at it, so actually walked a little towards another bus stop which I got to in time. I got on that bus and maybe switched again, but ultimately ended up on a route that goes along the North and East side of the Old City, passing the Mount of Olives. I got a nice "bus tour" of some areas that I might not feel as comfortable to walk in (unfortunately as is our present state of things anyway). It took me straight to the kotel plaza. Good to know this bus exists!

## At the Kotel

I texted Ben since we talked about meeting at the kotel earlier

<Photos glob="/src/assets/2026-08-05/193412-kotel-plaza-at-dusk/*.jpg" />

I am happy to note that I don't have many pictures of today, but do have nice memories of it.

Yair made plans to see Spiderman. We actually talked about how we were going to go together, but I also told some of the kids that I would wait to see it when the rest of the family is in Israel together, plus I remember not being in the mood to go to a movie, but I encouraged Yair to venture to the movie theater. We went together to Nocturno, and then he went on his way.

And then I went shopping and to walk around. As I recall, Moshe requested lemon juice and a few other things.
