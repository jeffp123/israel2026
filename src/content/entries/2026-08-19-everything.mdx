There is a unique thing in Israel that most of us notice while here. You can find everything here. I am not talking about supermarket everything, but rather so much diversity in culture.

Yesterday, I spent the day at a kibbutz where there were parrots in the trees, communal dining, beautiful houses, lots of ice cream and family warmth.

It really felt like heaven.

Then as we got back to Jerusalem, the candy stand alone was worth traveling for, at least for the children. My youngest ate a cup full of kosher gummy worms as we walked in the hot sun, and another one of my children bought a sun hat for 5 shekel, that’s about $1.50!

Sure, the bus didn’t stop at the bus stop but we discovered 78 isn’t the only number out there and took a ride into a different neighborhood meeting new Olim along the way and watching the sun start turning pink.

Later in the evening, we got to jam at an open mic night! I played some bass and keyboard, then carried the keyboard over to a guy in a wheelchair who really wanted to play Gm, in a rolling sort of way. Yes, I crashed the keyboard stand at his feet but everyone ran to help!

What I don’t have is enough sleep to keep going, hence the dissatisfaction of cancelling plans in the morning due to extreme fatigue. But hopefully hiking later and checking out a chocolate factory with the kids and my husband in the afternoon. Last time we were here, the chocolate we made melted on the bus, so time to try again, and this time, no waiting to eat it.
