## Shabbat Eicha

We went to the Great Synagog for Shabbat. Yair helped lead the way there, to this huge shul next to Prima Kings, since he recognized the streets from his previous walk to the gaming meetup.

I was rather exhausted, having gone to bed very late the previous night (playing Boss Monster) and having not napped most of Friday, like Yair and Nuriya did.

Still, we made it to shul. It was an interesting experience. Did not feel like a typical Israeli shul (whatever typical actually is). There was singing, with an actual choir.

I had been curious to go since we were here three years ago and right next to the Great Synagog [as we stayed ath the Prima Kings in 2023], but never went then. It is a beautiful building and really well put together tefilla experience. Just not my style, which is OK.

[A story: On the way out, I noticed a little room near the stairs and it sounded like water was running. I went to check it out and there was a small sink that that leaking and making a big puddle. I took it upon myself to try to tell somebody who would know what to do, so the shul is not left with a big mess in the morning. And despite Yair observing that there are many English speakers and I could just tell somebody in English, I insisted on speaking in Hebrew. I tried pointing and saying "teep teep, mabul!"" Eventually somebody at the shul asked to see it and I assume they knew what to do from there.]

We then came back and I put some of the food on the platta and we got ready to make kiddush. Ben was to come over as planned, and indeed showed up right around when we were starting the challah.

We had a nice Shabbat meal, played games, and rested.

Later, we went on a little walk with Nuriya and Ben, to "look for cats" as Nuriya wanted to do. In one of the courtyards somebody walked out who Ben recognized. They were talking and at some point introduced me. His voice sounded a little familiar. Ben said that this person has a youtube channel. I asked what it was and he said "Babble on Talmud". This was Sruli Rapps and I was a listener of his podcast! I was actually a little star struck. I gave him a big hug and told him how much I like his podcast and how I now use it as my "reward" for getting through a daf that I am learning, to review with his podcast. It was a fun moment!

[Note, I just listened to his podcast on Megilla, daf 13, earlier today. That is the day that I am posting this entry, October 14th.  I want to add that this was one of those really special experiences. To be in a country of 15 million people and to run into somebody on the first Shabbat of the trip who I have been listening to their podcast and learning from! I can picture the moment well.]

For Shabbat day, we all slept a little late. I decided I want to just focus on whatever the kids want to do, and not force them to go a shul. I was feeling like I needed more rest anyway, so we just chilled out at home, davened, made kiddush. At some point Ben came again, and we gamed more, as the kids wanted to do. And later in the day, Nuriya and I went to Gan Sacher.

After havdalah, Yair and Ben went on a small mission to get something that Nuriya would eat, which was Sam's Bagels. They brought back a really interesting bagel for me, consisting of tuna and cream cheese and olives and other things. It was good.

At some point while they were looking for bagels, Moshe texted me that I should look at the moon, so Nuriya and I looked for the moon.
