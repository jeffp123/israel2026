## Frying Pan Adventure

I slept until around 11am. Yair and Nuriya were still sleeping, so I was able to shower, daven, put some things away, and just go at a nice pace. By around 12:30pm, they were still sleeping, so I decided to venture out to find a frying pan and spatula and a mikveh to toivel it. I went out with earbuds in, catching up on my daily mishnayot, and wandered a bit, before searching google maps for a cookware store.

[Here is an interesting bird that I saw along the way, if you can find it in the pictures, and also two selfies. These are the only pictures that I took on the way to the store that sold dishware in Rechavia.]

<Photos glob="/src/assets/2026-07-30/130314-interesting-bird/*.jpg" />

I found one a few blocks away and bought a frying pan. The owner's two very young daughters were there and when I tried to pay and she realized I speak English, challenged them to say in English "cash or card", which they seemed happy about, but ultimately tried to speak to me in fast Hebrew.

With the frying pan in hand, I set out for a mikveh that was on the way back. I stopped at a cafe and had fun trying to order scrambled eggs and coffee, ending up with this amazing piece of toast stacked with some sort of dairy product, eggs, and I think coconut?

<Photos glob="/src/assets/2026-07-30/130326-cafe/*.jpg" />

[Note: This cafe would later be Yair's almost daily hangout. I do not actually know the name of it, but it was next to Nocturna.]

I then found the keilim mikveh.

<Photos glob="/src/assets/2026-07-30/141122-keilim-mikveh/*.jpg" />

I then stopped to get some eggs, plasticware, and a USB charger.

I returned back to the apartment, kids still sound asleep, and so now I set out the kasher the oven and stovetop.

<Photos glob="/src/assets/2026-07-30/151235-kashering-while-kids-still-sleeping/*.jpg" />

[Note: In retrospect it might have not been necessary to kasher anything, but I did so as a precaution.]

I used Claude AI to figure out how to start the self cleaning cycle and then started the cycle, then had some time to catch up on various things, put more things away, and just try to take care of myself.

Baruch Hashem, I am now feeling well. Yair is up. Nuriya still just getting up. The oven is self-cleaning. Various batteries are being charged. And I am ready to do whatever next thing we might do.

[After these things, and looking at my photos, I apparently went on a little walk at around 5:15pm, while Nuriya was still getting up. If I remember correctly, I had a thought that I would shop for a new Apple Watch at BUG, but ended up just walking around and appreciating Jerusalem. I think this was when I gave up on the idea of replacing my watch on this trip.]
