It is worth mentioning in this part of the photoblog that I had been feeling the signs of a recurrent hernia, and as of that point, still yet unidentified. The symptoms had been emerging since before the trip, but was particularly bothering me on-and-off over this first week in Israel, possibly from the walking ~20,000 steps per day or carrying luggage, etc. I had the thought that I must do something about it while I am in Israel because why wait to be back in America? So, I found a doctor who specializes in hernia repair and was in touch with him. His practice was in Tel Aviv, so I had the idea that I would stop there on the way the to airport to pick up Moshe.

We also had plans to go to the Biblical Zoo in Beit Shemesh in the afternoon, so it would be a day of travel outside of Jerusalem, and I had a lot on my mind with all of it. As a result, I didn't get as much sleep as I'd have liked and there was a late start for the next day's adventures.
