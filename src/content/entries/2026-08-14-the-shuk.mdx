*Dated here as August 14, though it may have been August 21.*

I don’t think it’s a coincidence that this very very crowded market in the center of Jerusalem is called that. It’s definitely not for the bashful, well at least if you plan to actually acquire some Shabbos food on Friday morning or afternoon. It’s for the “shook”. That is for *those quite clearly shaken out of their comfort zone* **people**, like I was.

Try not to get touched or lightly pushed while waiting your turn at the shnitzel stand, and then the guy says: “we are all out of shnitzel with no sesame seeds. “ Why does everyone like no sesame seeds too?!?

And even so, it’s Friday here in the quiet corner of America, and I am thinking about Shabbat prep and the shuk comes to mind.

It doesn’t seem so stressful from here, it seems lively :).
