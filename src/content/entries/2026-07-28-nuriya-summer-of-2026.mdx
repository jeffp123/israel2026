*Nuriya wrote this for her Alim class's November writing prompt (it covers the whole trip, so it's dated here at the start of it): "Write about a time when you went on vacation with your family." It's transcribed below exactly as she wrote it, and the original is pictured at the end.*

I had just finished Camp Shalom and was getting ready to go to Israel.

I had about a week to pack, maybe a little more. I packed my headphones, my sound machine light thingy, my stuffies, my hairbrush, clothes (of course), and I think a few other things but I forgot what. I’m pretty sure we left the house around 10:00am-ish but i don’t know. I have absolutely no idea what time we got to the airport but what I do know is that it took a reeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeealy long time to actually get on to the plane. When we finally did get on I played video games and watched videos for most of the time. My Dad wanted me to go to sleep and I tried a few times but the seats just weren’t comfy enough! I couldn’t fall asleep!

When we finally got to Israel, after about 10-11 long hours we had to take some buses and 1 or 2 trains then we finally got to our 1st air b’n’b. (p.s. It was just me, my dad and my oldest brother Yair. The rest of the family is coming later). We got to the air b’n’b around 12:30-1:30-ish. My Dad made me go to sleep as soon as we got there because I didn’t sleep on the plane. I woke up around 12:00 in the morning. I had some crackers and then went back to sleep -well, I tried to go back to sleep but... I couldn’t because, well I had just been sleeping for about 11-12 hours so I just read until about 5:00 am I tried going to sleep a few more times while I was reading but I couldn’t at around 5:00 I went to my desk and started to draw in my notebook while I watched the sunrise.

When my Dad and older brother, Yair woke up we went to some places. First, we went to get some sort of charger or something for Yair, then, my Dad and I walked around and went to random shops that looked cool. I didn’t want to get anything except this really cool and comfy pink chair but it cost too much so my Dad wouldn’t get it for me. ): (I think it was like 500 bucks) It was still a nice walk though we also went in to a bakery or two and a lot of toy stores. I forgot what we did when we got home. The next day we walked around a lot. That night I went to bed around 10:00pm and the next day I woke up around 6:00... pm!!! I walked around a bit with my Dad and we found a super yummy pizza place called Pinto’s Pizza and got pizza there, it was delicious!

I think about a week and maybe about... 2 days later we went to Telaviv and hung out there for the day. We went to this museum and they put a bunny on my lap (I think his name was Fredrick or something like that) and he scratched me. At night, we went to pick up my older brother, Moshe. He was on this program called Hatzallah in Israel but in a different part than where we were. Moshe was at the airport because he was saying bye to everybody else in the program because most of the other people were going back to America. Me and my brother were both very tired so when we were waiting for the bus to come and bring us home, Moshe lied down on the bench and he didn’t even push me off when I leaned on him! He must’ve been really tired.

A few days later we went to a new air b’n’b and my Dad had to wake up at like 4:45am so he could pick up my mom, my gramma, my grandpa, my older sister, Eshka, and my younger sister, Shira. When they got there my older sister, Eshka, made me switch to a different bed and that bed wasn’t as comfy. In the new air b’n’b we had a chicken whose name was Chedva, which is also my middle name. The chicken was a girl. As soon as Chedva saw me it started chasing me and I probably wouldn’t have been that scared but it had just pecked shira so yeah.

My grandparents were staying at a hotel called PRIMA KINGS. The bus that brought us from our air b’n’b to PRIMA KINGS was bus 78. It was about an hour - an hour ½ walk but we still walked there sometimes like if we went there before shabbos by bus, then at night, when we left, we would have to walk. Or the other way around, if we wanted to go there during shabbos day then we would walk there during the day and then wait for shabbos to be over and take a bus back. We went to this beach and in some spots the sand was like silk! Also the waves were the perfect size and it was just the best beach ever. We stayed there from around 7:00pm I think until about 10:00pm. On one of the last few days we saw this absolutely amazing musical lightshow. Maybe about a day or two later, we packed and got up the next day at like 4:00am-ish and left.

The End
